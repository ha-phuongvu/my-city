# Models

The GLB models the site shows, one file per card on the prototype's Models tab (for example
`fountain.glb`), exactly as TRELLIS or Tripo made them: full meshes and all their maps (color,
normal and metal/roughness), so they keep their detail.

1. Make the GLB from the card's picture with TRELLIS.2 or Tripo.
2. Save it here under the card's name.
3. Show it with `<Model src="/models/fountain.glb" />`, or `<ModelInstances>` for scenery that repeats.

They are large (about 2 MB each, and `book.glb` is 60 MB), well over the 5 MB first download in
NFR-3D-02.
